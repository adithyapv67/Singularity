01 · About Us

Engineering curiosity into capability.

Singularity

Singularity – The Astronomical Society of BMSCE is a student-driven community that brings together individuals passionate about exploring the universe and pushing the boundaries of science and technology. Our activities span from astronomy and deep-space phenomena to the practical applications of astronomy, robotics, and engineering. We regularly organize large-scale stargazing events, interactive podcasts and panel discussions with ISRO scientists and industry experts, industrial visits, ISRO visits, and engaging astronomy-focused events for the student community.

Alongside our outreach initiatives, Singularity has a dedicated technical team working on projects involving radio and optical telescopes, antenna design, radio astronomy, and related technologies. Our members actively present research at conferences such as WAMS, GEDS2, ASI, URSI, and IEEE conferences, while participating in technical competitions including SWANOTCH, SWAI, and the IEEE AP-S Student Design Contest. Building on this foundation, we have now ventured into rover engineering, forming Singularity's first dedicated rover team.

04 · The Challenge

Four Days. One Rover. Continuous Adaptation.

Hosted annually by Adelaide University at the Roseworthy Exterres Analogue Facility.

01

Design & Build

Develop a lunar-capable rover designed to tackle challenges inspired by the Australian Space Agency's Civil Space Strategy.

02

Autonomous Traversal

Implement semi- to fully autonomous traversal systems for navigating the simulated lunar terrain.

03

In-Situ Resource Utilisation

Develop practical skills and systems for lunar construction and resource gathering on the field.

04

Iterative Adaptation

Troubleshoot, adapt, and improve designs overnight to overcome the escalating challenges of the following day.
